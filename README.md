
# EnRoute: A Gamified Productivity Application Inspired by Istanbul's Public Transportation

## 1. Project Information

- **Project Name:** EnRoute
- **Project Type:** Graduation Project
- **Application Type:** Mobile Application
- **Planned Technologies:** Flutter, Dart, Git, and GitHub

---

## 2. Project Description
EnRoute is a mobile productivity tool designed to increase the enjoyment of learning new skills, working, and studying.
When working alone for extended periods of time, many people struggle to stay motivated and focused. 
Timers and task monitoring are common features of traditional productivity apps, but the process may grow monotonous with time. 
By transforming concentrated work periods into virtual trips influenced by Istanbul's public transit system, EnRoute takes a new tack.

On a virtual map modeled after Istanbul's metro, Metrobus, ferry, and Marmaray networks, users will be able to choose their departure and destination stations. 
The length of a focus session will depend on the chosen journey. 
Users will track their virtual progress between stations as they work till they arrive at their target. 
The goal of this journey-based experience is to increase the visibility of progress and provide a distinct sense of accomplishment for each session that is finished.

Gamification features will also be incorporated into the application. After finishing focus sessions, users will receive virtual Istanbulkart points. 
You can spend these points to personalize virtual transportation vehicles, unlock routes, and gather station stamps. 
Weekly tournaments, group prizes, simulated rush-hour challenges, and seated and standing options are among the other planned features.

The ability to start or join shared travels with friends or other users is another crucial feature. 
Instead of working completely alone, participants will be able to concentrate during the same virtual voyage, creating a shared experience. 
The goal is to add a social component without making the app a complete social networking site.

The primary target users are university students, young professionals, and individuals who want to manage their time while studying, working, learning new skills, or completing personal projects.
The application may also appeal to people who enjoy gamified experiences and want an alternative to conventional productivity timers.

The primary goal is to create, build, and assess a mobile application that integrates virtual transportation, prizes, shared focus sessions, and a customizable attention timer. 
In order to determine how people react to this approach's primary features, the study will examine its usability and gather user feedback. 
It won't presume that gamification inevitably increases concentration or output.

The first version will focus on user accounts, configurable focus sessions, virtual route and station progression, session history, a basic reward system, and shared journeys. 
Live public transportation tracking, real-time arrival information from transport operators, and a complete transportation management system are outside the initial scope.
Advanced social networking, complex league systems, extensive vehicle customization, and sophisticated anti-distraction mechanisms may be postponed to future versions. 
These limitations will help keep the project manageable within the graduation project timeline.

## 3. Initial Introduction

Maintaining focus is an important part of studying, working, and developing new skills.
However, working independently for long periods can sometimes feel repetitive, making it difficult to stay engaged and complete planned activities.

EnRoute is a mobile application that explores a journey-based approach to productivity. 
Inspired by Istanbul's public transportation network, it transforms focus sessions into virtual trips where users move between stations as they work. 
By combining visible progress, rewards, and shared journeys, the application aims to provide a more interactive way to organize focused work sessions.
The project will begin with the development of the core focus-session experience and will gradually introduce additional features according to their feasibility and the available development time.

---

## 4. Initial Problem Statement

People who study, work, or learn independently may struggle to maintain focus and motivation consistently. 
Although traditional productivity applications can help users manage time, their timer-based experience may not always feel engaging over repeated use.

There is an opportunity to explore alternative ways of presenting focus sessions through visible progress, meaningful rewards, and social participation.
EnRoute addresses this opportunity by representing focused work as a virtual journey through Istanbul's public transportation network.

The project will investigate whether this approach provides a usable and engaging alternative to a conventional focus timer.
Its effectiveness will be evaluated through functional testing and user feedback rather than assumed in advance.

---

## 5. Initial Motivation and Objective

### Motivation

EnRoute was designed around the natural idea that every journey has a starting point, a route, visible progress, and a destination. Applying this concept to productivity could transform a focus session from a simple countdown timer into a small journey with a clear goal.

The project also explores whether rewards and shared sessions can make working independently more engaging. Istanbul's public transportation network provides a familiar setting for this experience and creates opportunities for virtual routes, collectibles, and challenges.

### Objectives

The main objectives of EnRoute are to:

- Develop a mobile focus timer with customizable session durations.
- Create a virtual map and route progression inspired by Istanbul's public transportation network.
- Implement a basic points and rewards system.
- Allow users to create or join shared focus journeys.
- Track completed sessions and provide basic statistics.
- Test the main functionality and collect user feedback.

## 6. Academic References

### Reference 1: The Gamification of Learning: A Meta-analysis

- **Authors:** Michael Sailer and Lisa Homner
- **Year:** 2020
- **Publication:** *Educational Psychology Review*
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w

**What I learned:** This meta-analysis examines the effects of gamification on learning-related outcomes, including motivation and behavior. 
It provides a foundation for investigating features such as points, achievements, and visible progress in EnRoute. 
It also suggests that gamification should be designed carefully rather than assumed to work equally well in every context.

### Reference 2: The Impact of Gamification on Learning and Instruction: A Systematic Review of Empirical Evidence

- **Authors:** Zamzami Zainuddin, Samuel Kai Wah Chu, Muhammad Shujahat, and Corinne Jacqueline Perera
- **Year:** 2020
- **Publication:** *Educational Research Review*
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2020.100326

**What I learned:** This systematic review examines empirical research on gamification in learning and instruction. It is relevant to EnRoute because the project uses game elements to encourage participation in focused activities. It also reinforces the need to evaluate the actual user experience instead of assuming that adding rewards will automatically improve engagement.

## 9. Initial Development Plan and Evaluation

The planned mobile development framework is Flutter, using Dart. Git and GitHub will be used for version control and project documentation. 
The backend technology and database will be selected after defining the requirements for accounts, session history, rewards, and shared journeys.

Development will begin with the core focus timer and virtual journey experience. 
Shared journeys and additional gamification features will be introduced after the basic functionality is working.

The initial evaluation will include functional tests and user feedback. 
The main areas of evaluation will be ease of use, clarity of journey progress, and users' responses to the rewards and shared-session features.
